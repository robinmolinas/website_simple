# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, engineering and design leaders, venture capital partners, and startup founders seeking an expert who builds at the intersection of high-level business strategy and autonomous multi-agent AI systems.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Robin Molinas' exceptional talent, dual capabilities in strategy and development, and a proven track record of shipping complex AI platforms. The site must serve as concrete proof of front-end execution and design craft.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Measured, authority-driven, intellectually humble but deeply competent.
- **Tone**: Sophisticated, clean, direct.
- **3-Word Personality**: Expert, Understated, High-Performance.
- **Emotional Goals**: Instill absolute trust, respect for craft, and modern aesthetic delight within the first three seconds.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landings with repetitive "features card grids" (icon + heading + paragraph).
- Low-contrast light/dark gray standard Tailwind designs.
- Over-designed "creative-agency" sites that compromise readability, scroll performance, or accessibility for decoration.
- Decorative text gradients (`background-clip: text`) or gimmicky glassmorphism.

## Design Principles
1. **Build over slide-decking**: The interface itself should prove the technical ability to ship, not just explain it in text.
2. **Details define the premium**: Every corner radius, border-hover animation curve, typography tracking, and margin rhythm must feel calculated, deliberate, and high-craft.
3. **Understated sophistication**: Use deep neutral tones, high typographic contrast, and restrained accents to create weight rather than noise.
4. **Fluid physics**: Motion must represent real inertia and ease-out curves, feeling responsive and alive instead of elastic or floaty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **WCAG Level**: AA compliance.
- **Key Needs**: Text clarity and color contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. Semantic markup for screen readers. Respect user preference for reduced motion.
